Winter break 2026-27 · Bogota, Cartagena, Medellin · three travelers

Colombia Christmas 2026

Thursday December 17 to Monday January 4. Three nights in Bogota to land at altitude, six in Cartagena over Christmas, six in Medellin for New Year under the Alumbrados lights, two more in Bogota, home from the capital. Three short flights inside the country and no car leg over two hours.

Why this trip. Morocco priced at $16,500 to $22,000 for the same dates and Mexico only beat it with plain hotel rooms. Flexing the dates by a day found a $1,470 one-stop to Bogota, in the same bracket as the Mexico nonstop and $1,500 under Morocco for three. Colombia was fourth on the February shortlist and keeps its photography case: the walled city at dawn, Getsemani at night, Bazurto, the metrocable and Comuna 13.

Decisions so far. Bogota round trip on Avianca via San Salvador, Dec 17 to Jan 4 (Zoe misses only the minimum day). Cartagena for Christmas and Medellin for New Year, so the heat comes first and the lights last; the reverse costs more in Cartagena's New Year week. Guatape is an overnight option, not a day trip. Nothing is booked; both Cartagena and Medellin show most hotels sold out or not loaded for the holiday weeks.

Bogota

Fri Dec 18 – Sun Dec 20 · 3 nights · altitude first

FriDec 18

Land at 2,640 m, go slowly

Bogota

Land 11:35, which is 08:35 at home. Taxi or Uber 45 minutes to the hotel. A late lunch near the hotel, a flat walk, water, early dinner, early night. No Monserrate today: the altitude headache is real on day one.

Backup Altitude headache or a bad night on the plane: the Botero museum is two blocks from the Candelaria hotels and free; dinner at the hotel; nothing else.

SatDec 19

Candelaria at dawn, gold and Botero

Bogota

La Candelaria's streets at 07:00, doors, murals and the Plaza de Bolivar before the crowds. Gold Museum at opening, the Botero museum next door (free), lunch in the Centro. Rest. Zona G or Chapinero dinner.

Backup Rain in the Candelaria afternoon is normal in December; the Gold Museum is the indoor half-day and Monserrate moves to Sunday dusk.

SunDec 20

Flowers at dawn, Usaquen, Monserrate at dusk

Bogota

Paloquemao market at 06:00 for the flower aisles and a market breakfast. Usaquen Sunday flea market for lunch. Monserrate by funicular for sunset over the whole savanna (timed tickets, Sunday queues). Pack for the coast.

Backup Monserrate fogged in: Usaquen market and the Zona G dinner stand; the funicular queue on a Sunday is an hour, so buy timed tickets online.

Cartagena

Mon Dec 21 – Sat Dec 26 · 6 nights · Christmas

MonDec 21

Fly to the coast

Bogota → Cartagena

Slow morning, LATAM 15:05 → Cartagena 16:35 (1 h 30 m). Taxi 15 minutes to the walled city. First walk on the walls at sunset, dinner in the Centro.

Backup LATAM 15:05 missed: JetSMART 16:05 or the 06:15 next morning; the walled city at dusk is the only fixed point.

TueDec 22

Walled city dawn, Bazurto, Getsemani

Cartagena

Inside the walls at 06:00: Torre del Reloj, the San Diego balconies, the palenqueras setting up about 08:00 (pay for portraits, agree first). Bazurto market with a guide at 08:30 for the colour and the breakfast. Pool through the heat. The free nightly novena with music on the Plaza de los Coches at 18:00 (Dec 16 to 24, with a small Christmas market), then Plaza de la Trinidad in Getsemani and dinner there.

Backup Bazurto without a guide is not worth it; if the guide cancels, the Mercado de Getsemani and the palenqueras on Plaza de los Coches instead.

WedDec 23

Rosario Islands

Cartagena

Boat from the marina about 08:30 to a family day-pass club on the islands, lunch included, back by 16:00. The sea can be choppy with the December trade winds; a flat-calm day is not guaranteed. Early dinner.

Backup Rough sea (the brisa is strongest in December): skip the islands, pool morning, Castillo San Felipe moved here, Getsemani murals at 17:00.

ThuDec 24

Castillo, then Christmas Eve

Cartagena

Castillo San Felipe at the 08:00 opening before the heat, a slow afternoon, Christmas Eve dinner booked. The city parties late; fireworks at midnight from the walls.

Backup Christmas Eve table lost: Getsemani's Plaza de la Trinidad carts and a rooftop; the fireworks are everywhere at midnight.

FriDec 25

Christmas Day

Cartagena

A city asleep until noon. Pool, a long late lunch at a place that is open, the walls at sunset, Getsemani for the murals and a last plaza evening.

Backup Most of the city sleeps until noon. Pool, a late lunch at the hotel or a place from the list, the walls at sunset.

SatDec 26

Palenque, or the beach

Cartagena

San Basilio de Palenque, 1.5 hours inland (within the car rule): the first free African town in the Americas, drumming and the palenquera cooks; or a beach day at Bocagrande if the heat has won. Last Cartagena dinner.

Backup Heat: if the Palenque drive (1.5 h) feels like too much, Bocagrande beach or the hotel pool and a last dawn walk Sunday.

Medellin

Sun Dec 27 – Fri Jan 1 · 6 nights · New Year

SunDec 27

Fly to the mountains

Cartagena → Medellin

JetSMART or Avianca late morning to Medellin (1 h 15 m); Jose Maria Cordova airport is 45 to 60 minutes from El Poblado. Check in, Laureles or Poblado dinner, the first look at the Alumbrados lights.

Backup Flight delayed into Medellin: dinner in Poblado is a walk-in; the lights run every night until early January.

MonDec 28

Comuna 13 and the metrocable

Medellin

Comuna 13 with a guide at 09:00: the escalators, the murals, the hip-hop story, before the afternoon crowds. Metro to Acevedo and the Line K cable up to Santo Domingo for the aerial frames. Rest; lights after dark.

Backup Comuna 13 is a daylight-only, guide-only visit; rain moves it to Dec 30 and the Jardin Botanico takes its place.

TueDec 29

Guatape overnight, or Parque Arvi

Medellin → Guatape (option)

Option: 2 hours by road to Guatape for the striped zocalos and El Penol's 740 steps at dusk, one night by the lake, back the next morning. Without it: Parque Arvi and its Line L cable are closed on Tuesdays, so take Line K at first light (it runs from 04:30) over the hillside barrios, then Pueblito Paisa on Cerro Nutibara and a Laureles evening.

Backup Guatape overnight dropped: Arvi is shut on Tuesdays, so a dawn ride on cable Line K, Pueblito Paisa and a slow Laureles evening.

WedDec 30

Botero, the garden, the lights

Medellin

Plaza Botero at 09:00 and the Museo de Antioquia, the Jardin Botanico for lunch under the orchid canopy, and the Alumbrados along the river from 18:30 with the tripod.

Backup Museo de Antioquia closed for the holiday week: Plaza Botero at 09:00 is the photograph anyway; lights at 18:30.

ThuDec 31

New Year's Eve

Medellin

Laureles slow day, Mercado del Rio or a cafe. Medellin's New Year is a family night with the 12 grapes and the burning of the ano viejo dolls; dinner booked early. Fireworks are banned in the city and set off everywhere anyway, so watch from the hotel terrace or Cerro Nutibara. The metro stops at 22:00, so plan the taxi.

Backup No reservation: Mercado del Rio food hall at 13:00 for a long lunch and a quiet evening; Medellin's New Year is at home with fireworks at midnight.

FriJan 1

The holiday

Medellin

Everything official is shut. A late start, the river lights one more time, Laureles parks, a late lunch at a place that opens. Pack.

Backup Everything official is shut; the river lights, Laureles parks and a late lunch at a hotel restaurant are the day.

Where to eat

Researched Oct 10 · tables with sources in reports/Restaurants 2026 - Bogota.md and - Cartagena.md

One traveler is vegetarian. The traps are beef-rib stock in soups and the sopa del dia, chicken in ajiaco, tocino in frijoles and lentejas, meat inside empanadas, arepas de huevo and carimanolas, ant butter in some modern Bogota kitchens, and on the coast fish stock and shared fryers. The script: Soy vegetariano, no como carne, pollo ni pescado. El caldo es de carne o de verduras? Lleva tocino? Prices in pesos, about 3,200 to the dollar. The Cartagena pass covered six restaurants before a usage limit stopped it, and the Medellin pass did not finish, so those sections are short and the Medellin names are unverified.

Bogota Dec 18–20 and Jan 2–3

Fri Dec 18 · late lunch after landing

Abasto Quinta Camacho, Calle 69A 9-09

The best all-round family restaurant on the list: arepa de choclo with mozzarella, huevos en cazuela, criolla-potato gnocchi, a portobello burger, carrot soup with goat cheese. Open to 16:00 weekdays. The sopa de tortilla is chicken stock.

Fri Dec 18 · early dinner

Salvo Patria, Carrera 4 bis 58-60, Chapinero Alto

A dedicated "Vegetal" section: charred cabbage with miso butter and quinoa tabbouleh, a barley and mushroom risotto with Sabana cheeses, beets with goat cheese, native-bean hummus. The corn agnolotti and the cubio pate use ant butter. COP 70,000–110,000, kombucha for Zoe, salvopatria.precompro.com.

Sat Dec 19 · breakfast

La Puerta Falsa, Calle 11 6-50, by the cathedral

Since 1816: chocolate completo with a slab of cheese dropped in, bread and almojabana, about COP 12,000–16,000; changua (milk, egg, scallion, no meat stock) about 12,500. The tamal and ajiaco are meat. Six tables; go before 9:00.

Sat Dec 19 · Centro lunch

Quinua y Amaranto, or Maria Candela Vegan

Quinua y Amaranto by the Chorro de Quevedo does a vegetarian set lunch with soup and juice for about COP 20,000–25,000; Maria Candela near the Botero museum has the one documented meat-free ajiaco in the Centro. Hours unverified, check the day before.

Sat Dec 19 · dinner

Mini-Mal, Transversal 4 bis 56A-52

Small and calm, with labelled vegetarian dishes: the quinoa starter, the campesina salad, Sopa Samai vegetable broth, mushrooms in green curry, almojabana cake. The Sopa Citrica is dried shrimp. Phone (601) 455 0630; closed Mondays and holidays.

Sun Dec 20 · market breakfast

Paloquemao, Av. Calle 19 25-04

The fruit aisles (granadilla, pitahaya, uchuva), fresh cheese, arepa de choclo, pandebono and almojabana, fresh juices; Don Camilo's arepa vegetariana (confirm no chicharron). All the soups and the tamal are meat. Flower hall before 9:00.

Sun Dec 20 · Usaquen lunch, Sunday supper

Abasto Usaquen, Calle 118 5-41

One block from the flea market, Sunday to 16:30, fills by 12:30 (WhatsApp 315 871 5220); the zapallo arroz caldoso is the dish. Chapinero kitchens mostly close by 17:00 on Sundays, so supper is Wok on Calle 69 (ask sin salsa de pescado), Crepes & Waffles or Cantina y Punto.

Monserrate at dusk (Fri or Sat; it closes 17:00 Sundays)

Casa Santa Clara at the top

The vegetarian order is the onces: hot chocolate, almojabana, cuajada with melao. Neither restaurant at the top has a documented vegetarian main, so come down by 19:30 for dinner in Chapinero.

Sat Jan 2 · dinner

El Chato's ground-floor bistro, Calle 65 4-76

Latin America's 50 Best number one in 2025; the a la carte downstairs states vegetarian options at COP 24,000–130,000 a plate. The upstairs tasting menu (COP 710,000) is not for this party. WhatsApp +57 321 971 8676.

Mon Jan 4 · before the flight

Masa, Calle 70 4-83, the day before

Buy almojabanas and pan de yuca for the airport; at 06:30 only the 24-hour Canon del Chicamocha (Calle 57 19-09, changua) or the hotel is open.

Cartagena Dec 21–27 · six restaurants verified

Christmas Eve or Day, and the best vegetarian table

Alma, Hotel Casa San Agustin, Calle de la Universidad 36-44

A labelled vegan section: chickpea bowl with lentil croquettes and palm hearts, grilled cauliflower with a coconut-corn tamal, mushroom wild rice with portobello. Courtyard, live son on weekends, fine for Zoe. As a hotel restaurant it is the likeliest to be open Dec 24 and 25. Book now at alma.precompro.com.

The special dinner

Carmen, Calle del Santisimo 38-19, San Diego

Caribe salad with burrata and cashews, a mushroom "tartar" with black garlic, a spiced native-bean steak; the empanadas are shellfish and the tasting menus have no printed vegetarian version. One of the hardest tables December to March; WhatsApp +57 310 394 9344. No lunch Monday or Tuesday.

The cheap local lunch

La Mulata, Calle del Quero 9-58, San Diego

Set lunch around COP 30,000; ask for the vegan coconut rice with vegetables and plantain and swap the fish soup that starts every set meal. Walk in at 12:00 before the office queue.

Getsemani

Celele and Dona Lola

Celele (Latin America's 50 Best no. 5) for one adventurous lunch, ordering a la carte: its set menus are stated as not suitable for vegetarians; closed Sundays. Dona Lola in Casa Lola is the easy option with a rooftop bar; its menu reportedly marks vegetarian dishes, unverified.

For the fish eaters

La Cevicheria, Calle Stuart 7-14

Bourdain's ceviche bar; the vegetarian gets one salad and dessert. No reservations; walk in 15:00–17:00.
