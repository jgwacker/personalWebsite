Istanbul

Sat Apr 3 – Tue Apr 6 · 3 nights

SatApr 3

Land, walk, eat, sleep

Istanbul

Land 17:45, which is 07:45 at home. Transfer to Sultanahmet and drop bags. Walk the square at sunset for Zoe's first look at the Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia, then a simple dinner close to the hotel: pide, or lamb at Hamdi over the Golden Horn. No naps on arrival. Lights out by 22:00 local.

SunApr 4

Zoe's greatest hits

Istanbul

Up with the light and out for a Turkish breakfast by 08:00; morning sun is the fastest reset. Basilica Cistern at 09:00 opening, Hagia Sophia gallery, Blue Mosque, done by lunch. Fish sandwich at Eminönü, where the ferry crowds, simit sellers and gulls are the street-life shoot. Afternoon ferry to Kadıköy for the fish and produce market streets with a camera, and an early dinner at Çiya Sofrası. Ferry back at sunset for the skyline. The Grand Bazaar is closed Sundays, so it waits for tomorrow.

MonApr 5

Your pick, and a long lunch

Istanbul

Sleep until you wake. Breakfast in Karaköy, baklava at Güllüoğlu on the way. Grand Bazaar as it opens, before the tour groups, for the shopkeepers setting up, then the hans and side streets around it. Afternoon on the Golden Horn: Balat's painted streets and corner cafés, the Rahmi Koç submarine for Zoe, Pierre Loti at sunset. Dinner somewhere you already love.

TueApr 6

Slow morning, big lunch, night flight

Istanbul

Sleep in. Spice Bazaar and the wholesale streets behind it toward Süleymaniye, which are better street photography than the bazaar itself. A proper lunch, since the next real meal is Khiva breakfast: kuru fasulye below Süleymaniye or kebap at Zübeyir in Beyoğlu. Late checkout, bags at the hotel, leave Sultanahmet about 16:30. TK to Urgench departs 20:20. Istanbul is only two hours behind Uzbekistan, so the hard part of the time shift is already done.

Khiva

Wed Apr 7 – Thu Apr 8 · 2 nights

WedApr 7

Arrive before dawn

Khiva

Land Urgench 02:05, fixer drives 30 min to Itchan Kala, check in about 03:00 (room booked from the night of the 6th). Sleep late. Afternoon walk inside the walls, sunset from the west wall or the hotel rooftop.

ThuApr 8

Walls and minarets

Khiva

Dawn about 06:30 at Kunya Ark and the walls. Islam Khoja minaret after 08:00 opening (narrow spiral stair, lookout at 45 m). Juma Mosque's forest of carved pillars. Rest in the afternoon. Sunset from the west wall.

Bukhara

Fri Apr 9 – Sun Apr 11 · 3 nights

FriApr 9

Train to Bukhara

Khiva → Bukhara

Dawn shoot and breakfast in Khiva. Train 707 departs 09:55, arrives Bukhara 14:32; be at the station by 09:15 for the bag check. Flat desert out the window for four and a half hours, lunch from the Khiva bakeries. Fixer meets you at Kagan station, 15 minutes from the old town. Check in, then Poi Kalyan at blue hour.

SatApr 10

Poi Kalyan at dawn

Bukhara

Dawn at Poi Kalyan. The Ark and Bolo Hauz mosque. Afternoon rest. Evening around Lyab-i Hauz.

SunApr 11

Outer Bukhara

Bukhara

Chor Minor at dawn. Sitorai Mokhi-Khosa summer palace and the Chor-Bakr necropolis. Trading domes and a craft workshop in the afternoon.

Samarkand

Mon Apr 12 – Wed Apr 14 · 3 nights

MonApr 12

Fast train east

Bukhara → Samarkand

Morning Afrosiyob, 1.5 h. Afternoon at Gur-e-Amir. Registan exterior at blue hour from the free steps. No sound-and-light show on Mondays.

TueApr 13

Registan day

Samarkand

Dawn on the Registan exterior. One paid entry mid-morning for the courtyards (the ticket is single-entry per day). Siab bazaar and Bibi-Khanym. Shah-i-Zinda in late-afternoon west light. Lights come on about 20:00, show about 20:30, watched from the free steps.

WedApr 14

Shah-i-Zinda at opening

Samarkand

Shah-i-Zinda as the gates open. Ulugbek observatory. Konigil paper mill. Rest. Optional second show night.

What to do next

In order

  • Now. Price the multi-city ticket on turkishairlines.com for three, confirm the 20:20 Urgench flight number and the fare family (EcoFly carries one checked bag), and buy.
  • Now. Check all three passports: valid through October 2027 with two blank pages.
  • After ticketing. Request the Istanbul stopover hotel through TK's tool; accept only a central assignment, otherwise book a Sultanahmet roof-terrace hotel for all three nights.
  • Now, via the fixer. Reserve family or triple rooms inside Itchan Kala, in Bukhara's old town near Lyab-i Hauz, in Samarkand near the Registan, and in Tashkent near Chorsu. Get a written quote for guide and driver days; no long transfers, the only drives are airports, stations and Chimgan.
  • Late February 2027. Fixer buys train tickets the morning sales open: Khiva to Bukhara train 707 for April 9, and the Afrosiyob for April 12 and April 15.
  • Early March 2027. Re-verify every flight time, read the State Department pages for Türkiye and Uzbekistan, enroll in STEP, ask the fixer for Registan hours and the drone decree status (assume drones stay banned).
  • Two weeks out. Timed tickets for the Basilica Cistern (Mon 09:00), eSIM installed before leaving the US, notarized consent letter and birth certificate copy for the minor, CDC vaccine check.
