Nutcracker Night

Painted Ladies at sunset, a walk down Hayes Street, dinner in Hayes Valley, and Zoe's first night at the Opera House.

Booked: Nutcracker tickets (on the phone) and a Mano at 5:45. Nothing else to reserve. December evening will be in the low 50s; bring a real coat for the hilltop and the walk home.

The route

About 1 mile on foot, all downhill or flat

3:3045 min

Alamo Square

Hayes St & Steiner St · top of the hill

The Painted Ladies are the row of Victorians on Steiner between Hayes and Grove, best seen from the slope above them with downtown behind. Walk the park's upper loop, then settle on the east side for the sunset at 4:50. The city lights come up fast in December, so stay until about 5:00 for the full effect.

5:0015 min walk

Down Hayes Street

Hayes St east from Steiner to Laguna · 0.7 mile, gentle downhill

Straight east on Hayes, past Fillmore, into Hayes Valley at Laguna. The neighborhood runs along Hayes between Laguna and Franklin, with Octavia and Patricia's Green in the middle.

5:1530 min

Hayes Valley shops

Most boutiques close at 6:00 on Saturdays, so this is a fast loop. Pick two or three:

  • Reliquary, 544 Hayes. Clothing with a curated vintage rack, the Haight thrift instinct at a higher grade.
  • Timbuk2, 506 Hayes. The SF bag company's flagship; custom bags built in the store.
  • Marine Layer, Hayes. SF-born soft basics, easy for a teenager.
  • Miette, 449 Octavia. Pastel candy shop, sweets sold by the piece. Usually open a little later.
  • Christopher Elbow Chocolates, 401 Hayes. The hot chocolate is the December move; open until about 7.
  • Isotope Comics, 326 Fell. One of the best comic shops in the country, with couches and a browser-friendly staff. Two blocks off the route and open into the evening, so it also works after dinner.
  • Patricia's Green and Proxy, Octavia at Hayes. Public art and shipping-container shops, free to wander.
5:4575 min

Dinner at a ManoReserved

450 Hayes St, between Gough and Octavia · reserved 5:45 for 2

Casual Italian: house-made pastas, pizzas, a tiled bar with red stools. Pastas adapt to vegan on request, so say so when ordering. Tell the server about the 7:00 curtain when seated; the Opera House is a four-minute walk and they turn pre-ballet tables all December. Aim to pay by 6:45.

7:00About 2 hrs

SF Ballet, NutcrackerTickets bought

War Memorial Opera House, 301 Van Ness Ave at Grove

Helgi Tomasson's production is set in San Francisco during the 1915 Panama-Pacific Exposition, so the party scene is a Pacific Heights Victorian and the Land of Sweets is the fair's Palace of Fine Arts. Act I is 47 minutes, intermission 20, Act II 50; curtain down about 9:00.

  • Doors open about an hour before. Be inside by 6:45; latecomers wait for a pause in the music.
  • Tickets are on the phone. Bags must fit under the seat or on a lap (about 14 by 14 by 6 inches). No photos or video during the performance.
  • Dress is whatever feels like an occasion. Anything from jeans to a dress is normal at Nutcracker.
  • The intermission bar sells hot chocolate and sweets. Lines are long; the lobby balconies and the view of City Hall's lit dome are the better use of 20 minutes.
9:05Home

Getting home

Zoe: pickup on the Grove Street side of the Opera House, Van Ness & Grove, around 9:05. Traffic on Van Ness after a show is slow; Franklin or Gough are easier for the approach.

Imani: Van Ness Muni station is across the street. Outbound N Judah to the Outer Sunset, about 35 minutes. Trains run every 10 to 12 minutes at that hour.

Logistics

Imani getting there

From the Outer Sunset, the 7 Haight/Noriega bus to Haight & Steiner, then six short blocks north to Alamo Square (about 10 minutes), roughly 40 minutes total. Or the N Judah to Van Ness, then the 21 Hayes bus west to Steiner.

Zoe drop-off

Hayes & Steiner at 3:30. Street parking around Alamo Square is tight on weekends; a quick drop at the corner is easiest.

Weather and gear

Early December in SF: highs around 58, falling to the low 50s by sunset, with wind on the hilltop. Real coat, closed shoes, something for the ears. Rain is possible in December; if it rains, skip the hilltop and start at the shops at 4:30.

If something slips

  • Running late to dinner: a Mano holds tables 15 minutes; call 415-506-7401.
  • Shops already closed: Christopher Elbow and Isotope stay open latest.
  • Rain: Hayes Valley shops from 4:30, dinner, ballet. The sunset is the only part that needs weather.
