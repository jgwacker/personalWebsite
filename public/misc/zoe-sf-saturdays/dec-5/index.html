Alamo Square

Hayes St & Steiner St · top of the hill

The Painted Ladies are the row of Victorians on Steiner between Hayes and Grove, best seen from the slope above them with downtown behind. Walk the park's upper loop, then settle on the east side for the sunset at 4:50. The city lights come up fast in December, so stay until about 5:00 for the full effect.

5:0015 min walk

Down Hayes Street

Hayes St east from Steiner to Laguna · 0.7 mile, gentle downhill

Straight east on Hayes, past Fillmore, into Hayes Valley at Laguna. The neighborhood runs along Hayes between Laguna and Franklin, with Octavia and Patricia's Green in the middle.

5:1530 min

Hayes Valley shops

Everything here closes between 6:00 and 7:00 on Saturdays, so this is a fast loop before dinner. Pick two or three. Saturday closing times as listed; the two marked unconfirmed are from directory listings, not the stores.

  • Aviator Nation, 520 Hayes at Octavia. Surf-brand hoodies and sweats in rainbow stripes. Closes about 7:00, unconfirmed. Probably Zoe's first stop.
  • Timbuk2, 506 Hayes. Bags and backpacks, the SF company's flagship with custom bags built in the store. Closes 7:00.
  • Reliquary, 544 Hayes at Octavia. Women's clothing and jewelry with a curated vintage rack. Closes 7:00.
  • Marine Layer, 498 Hayes. Soft casual clothing, SF-born. Closes 6:00 or 7:00, unconfirmed.
  • Christopher Elbow Chocolates, 401 Hayes at Gough. Chocolate shop with a hot chocolate bar, the December move. Listings say 6:00 or 8:00; call 415-355-1105 if it matters.
  • Isotope Comics, 326 Fell between Gough and Octavia. Comic book lounge, one of the best in the country, with couches. Closes 6:00, so it has to come before dinner.
  • Patricia's Green and Proxy, Octavia at Hayes. Public art, a playground and shipping-container shops, open anytime.

Dinner at a ManoReserved

450 Hayes St, between Gough and Octavia · reserved 5:45 for 2

Casual Italian: house-made pastas, pizzas, a tiled bar with red stools. Marine Layer is next door at 498 if there is a minute to spare. Pastas adapt to vegan on request, so say so when ordering. Tell the server about the 7:00 curtain when seated; the Opera House is a four-minute walk and they turn pre-ballet tables all December. Aim to pay by 6:45.

SF Ballet, NutcrackerTickets bought

War Memorial Opera House, 301 Van Ness Ave at Grove

Helgi Tomasson's production is set in San Francisco during the 1915 Panama-Pacific Exposition, so the party scene is a Pacific Heights Victorian and the Land of Sweets is the fair's Palace of Fine Arts. Act I is 47 minutes, intermission 20, Act II 50; curtain down about 9:00.

  • Doors open about an hour before. Be inside by 6:45; latecomers wait for a pause in the music.
  • Tickets are on the phone. Bags must fit under the seat or on a lap (about 14 by 14 by 6 inches). No photos or video during the performance.
  • Dress is whatever feels like an occasion. Anything from jeans to a dress is normal at Nutcracker.
  • The intermission bar sells hot chocolate and sweets. Lines are long; the lobby balconies and the view of City Hall's lit dome are the better use of 20 minutes.
9:05Meet

Meet at City Hall plaza, then home

Meet at City Hall plaza. From the Opera House, cross Van Ness and walk through the front of City Hall to Civic Center Plaza on the Polk Street side, about five minutes, with the lit dome overhead the whole way. Pickup for both at Polk & Grove around 9:10. Polk is far easier to reach than Van Ness after a show. Drop Imani in the Outer Sunset on the way, about 20 minutes out Fell and Lincoln, then home.

Logistics

Meeting up

Imani makes her own way to Alamo Square; we drive her home afterward. Meet at the Hayes & Steiner corner at 3:30.

Zoe drop-off

Hayes & Steiner at 3:30. Street parking around Alamo Square is tight on weekends; a quick drop at the corner is easiest.

Weather and gear

Early December in SF: highs around 58, falling to the low 50s by sunset, with wind on the hilltop. Real coat, closed shoes, something for the ears. Rain is possible in December; if it rains, skip the hilltop and start at the shops at 4:30.

If something slips

  • Running late to dinner: a Mano holds tables 15 minutes; call 415-506-7401.
  • Shops already closed: Timbuk2, Reliquary and probably Aviator Nation run to 7:00; Christopher Elbow may run to 8:00.
  • Rain: Hayes Valley shops from 4:30, dinner, ballet. The sunset is the only part that needs weather.
