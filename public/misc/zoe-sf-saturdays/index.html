The shape of the year

Museums

The four must-dos: SFMOMA, de Young, Legion of Honor, Exploratorium. All close at 5 on Saturdays, so each is an early-start day. de Young and the Legion are free to Bay Area residents on Saturdays.

Skipped: Cal Academy (Zoe has been five or six times) and the Asian Art Museum (visited spring 2026).

Performing arts

  • Ballet: Nutcracker Dec 5, Jewels Feb 27
  • Theater: The Bad News Bears at A.C.T., Mar 13
  • Musical: The Great Gatsby, May 8
  • Opera: Tosca, Jun 12
  • Live music: Biscuits & Blues, done; Sheba, pending

Neighborhoods

Done: Haight, Valencia, North Beach, Union Square. Ahead: Hayes Valley, Japantown, Outer Richmond, Chinatown, Inner Sunset, Embarcadero, SoMa, Fillmore, Presidio.
