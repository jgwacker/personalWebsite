Belize Christmas 2026

Friday December 18 to Monday January 4. Four nights in the Cayo jungle with one night inside Tikal across the Guatemalan border, five in the Garifuna village of Hopkins over Christmas, seven on Ambergris Caye for the reef, resort time and New Year's fireworks. An optional boat to Bacalar in Mexico replaces the last three nights.

Why this trip. Belize had the cheapest flight of every destination priced for these dates, under Oaxaca and Colombia. It gives the jungle, the Maya sites, the reef and a land border to Tikal in one small, English-speaking country. The catch is the ground: beach hotels at Christmas and guided days at $100–150 a person make it cost about what Mexico or Morocco does.

Decisions so far. American round trip, Dec 18 to Jan 4, home the same evening. Tikal as an overnight by road, for the 4:00 sunrise tour. Hopkins rather than Placencia: closer to Cayo, the Garifuna Christmas and drumming, Cockscomb next door, Dangriga's Jankunu festival on Dec 27. Ambergris rather than Caye Caulker for the resort stay and the fireworks. The drone stays home: Belize bans personal drones. Nothing is booked; 87–95% of well-reviewed places show unavailable.

Cayo

Fri Dec 18 – Sun Dec 20, and Tue Dec 22 · 4 nights · jungle and caves

FriDec 18

Fly in, drive to the jungle

SFO → Belize City → Cayo

American 05:30 via Dallas lands 14:55, which is 12:55 at home (Belize is two hours ahead). Private transfer from the airport, about 2 hours from US$70, into Cayo by about 17:30 as the light goes (sunset is about 17:30). Dinner at the lodge, early night.

Backup Flight late into BZE: the drive after dark is the thing to avoid, so sleep near the airport and drive at 07:00 with a Belize Zoo stop.

SatDec 19

Xunantunich, the Saturday market, Cahal Pech

Cayo

Xunantunich at opening: the hand-crank ferry over the Mopan, El Castillo before the heat. San Ignacio's Saturday market, the biggest of the week, for lunch. Rest. Cahal Pech on the hill above town for sunset.

Backup Rain: the Saturday market and the Green Iguana project in San Ignacio; Xunantunich moves to Tuesday afternoon.

SunDec 20

ATM cave

Cayo

Actun Tunichil Muknal with a licensed guide: wading, swimming and climbing to the crystal maiden. No cameras of any kind are allowed inside. Back mid-afternoon. Dinner booked at Guava Limb Café.

Backup ATM closed after heavy rain or too much for the day: cave tubing at Nohoch Che'en or the Barton Creek canoe cave.

Tikal

Mon Dec 21 – Tue Dec 22 · 1 night · Guatemala

MonDec 21

Across the border to Tikal

Cayo → Tikal

Early drive to the Benque Viejo / Melchor border (US$20 per person to leave Belize, cash), about 2–3 hours door to door to Tikal. Afternoon in the park, Temple of the Great Jaguar at golden hour, night at the Jungle Lodge inside the gate.

Backup A slow border: Flores for blue hour on the lake and a 03:00 start for the sunrise from there.

TueDec 22

Sunrise from Temple IV

Tikal → Cayo

The sunrise tour leaves the gate at 04:00 with a mandatory guide (Q100 sunrise pass on top of the Q150 day pass); Temple IV by 05:30, howler monkeys at first light, the Grand Plaza empty. Back over the border to Cayo by mid-afternoon; pool, early night.

Backup Sunrise fogged in (common): stay for the morning wildlife on the plazas and leave at noon.

Hopkins

Wed Dec 23 – Mon Dec 28 · 5 nights · Christmas

WedDec 23

The Hummingbird Highway

Cayo → Hopkins

About 2–2.5 hours through the citrus valleys, a swim at St. Herman's Blue Hole on the way. Hopkins by lunch, the beach. Driftwood is closed on Wednesdays.

ThuDec 24

Reef morning, Christmas Eve

Hopkins

A snorkel morning on the reef off Hopkins [confirm operator]. Garifuna drumming at Lebeha in the afternoon. Christmas Eve dinner booked at Chef Rob's; midnight Mass in the village if you want it.

Backup Northers cancel the boat: drumming lesson at Lebeha and a beach morning.

FriDec 25

Christmas Day

Hopkins

A public holiday: local places close. Beach, resort lunch, sunset walk through the village.

SatDec 26

Cockscomb jaguar reserve

Hopkins

Boxing Day: Cockscomb Basin, 20–30 minutes away: Ben's Bluff trail, the waterfall pool, tubing the river. Jaguars are rarely seen; tracks are common. Driftwood's Saturday drumming at night.

Backup Boxing Day closures at Cockscomb: Mayflower Bocawina falls instead.

SunDec 27

Jankunu in Dangriga

Hopkins

The Garifuna masked dance festival in Dangriga, 30 minutes away, on the last Sunday after Christmas [confirm date and time]. Dancers in masks and feathered crowns, drums, crowds; a photographer's day.

Backup No Jankunu in Dangriga this year or a date change: a quiet beach day and Driftwood drumming the night before.

Hol Chan and Shark Ray Alley

Ambergris Caye

The marine reserve by boat, about US$113 per person, turtles and nurse sharks in waist-deep water. Afternoon off.

ThuDec 31

Slow day, dinner booked at Hidden Treasure, the official fireworks over San Pedro at midnight. Option: the 08:30 boat to Chetumal for three nights in Bacalar instead.

New Year's Day

Public holiday; resort and beach.

SatJan 2

Blue Hole from the air, or Caye Caulker

Ambergris Caye

A small-plane flight over the Great Blue Hole, about US$250–300 per person and better for a 12-year-old than the long boat day; or the water taxi to Caye Caulker for the Split and a slow lunch. Weather buffer for the reef.

Backup Blue Hole flight grounded: the Caye Caulker water taxi day instead.

SunJan 3

Ambergris Caye

Lamanai by boat and river, an 8-hour day to the temples among the howler monkeys (about US$140–169 per person), or a golf cart to Secret Beach. Elvi's and El Fogon are closed Sundays. Pack.

Where to eat

Researched Oct 11 · sources in reports/Restaurants 2026.md

Mira is vegetarian. English works everywhere in Belize, so ask the exact question: rice and beans are often boiled with pig tail or salt beef, refried beans and tamales use lard, panades are usually fish, and hudut, sere and tapado are fish stews. In Guatemala say sin carne, sin manteca; ¿los frijoles tienen manteca? Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year’s Day are public holidays and local cook shops close, so plan resort dinners. No restaurant has posted 2026 holiday hours yet.
