Morocco Winter 2026

Sunday December 13 to Sunday January 10. A work week in Rabat to land and get over jet lag, then the laptops go into storage for sixteen days: Marrakech, the Atlantic at Essaouira, Christmas back in Marrakech, three days over the Atlas to the Erg Chebbi dunes, New Year's Eve in Fes, Chefchaouen and Tangier. Then a second work week in Rabat and home from Casablanca.

Why this version. The Christmas-week fare to Casablanca was $2,336–2,412 a person. Leaving on Sunday Dec 13 and coming home Sunday Jan 10 drops it to $979–1,107, about $4,000 less for three. Working from Rabat in local hours for the two weeks either side of the break means no extra days off, and the extra nights cost roughly what living at home does.

Decisions so far. Delta / Air France via Boston and Paris; Rabat as the work base at both ends, with the work laptops stored there during the tour; Essaouira added now that there is time; the Sahara with one long drive day each side; the 3.5-hour Fes to Chefchaouen car accepted. Still open: Zoe's independent-study approval for Dec 14–18 and Jan 4–8, both employers' approval to work from Morocco, and written confirmation that the Rabat hotel will hold the laptops. Nothing is booked.

The desert road

Sat Dec 26 – Tue Dec 29 · Dades, a camp, Merzouga

Marrakech → Dades

Driver at 08:00. The Tizi n'Tichka pass at 2,260 m, Ait Benhaddou on foot about 11:30, lunch at Ouarzazate or Skoura, the Valley of the Roses, the Dades switchbacks in late light. About six and a half hours of driving; dinner at the hotel.

Where to eat

Researched Oct 10 · full tables with hours, phones and sources in reports/Restaurants 2026 - *.md

One traveler is vegetarian, so every entry names the dish to order. The standing traps: harira is made on meat stock, "vegetable" tagines and couscous are often cooked in the meat broth, pastilla is never vegetarian, and smen (aged butter) hides in couscous. Say it in French and Darija: sans viande, sans bouillon de viande; bla l-ham, bla d-jaj, bla hoot; wash fee-ha merqat l-ham? (is there meat broth in it?). Hours and closed days were checked against the day plan; items marked "confirm" rest on a single source.

Msemen, baghrir and bissara from dawn to noon, 5–10 MAD each; the Mellah sfenj stall; Pâtisserie des Princes from 06:00. Orange juice on the square: local price about 5 MAD, pay before they pour, watch it squeezed, refuse jug-poured glasses.

The square at night

Jemaa el-Fna stalls

Eat snacks there and dinner elsewhere: harira is lamb-based, bread, olives and water are charged even untouched, and menus with prices are mandatory, so ask for one. Agree snake and monkey prices before the camera comes out, or walk on.

Essaouira Dec 21–24

Mon Dec 21 · dinner

Triskala, 58 Rue Touahen (north medina, by the ramparts)

Mira orders the falafel with kemia, the veggie tapas, or the pumpkin-and-artichoke vegetarian tajine; only the soup needs checking, since it may be fish stock. Open daily 12:30–15:30 and 18:30–23:00; book by phone the day before (+212 808 548 268), about 100–200 MAD [confirm].

Tue Dec 22 · lunch

Port grilled-fish stalls, inside the port gate off Place Moulay Hassan

Mira has bread and salade marocaine at the stall (frites share the fish fryer [confirm]; the soup is fish), or a real meal at Pâtisserie Driss two minutes away. Agree the price per kilo and the grill fee before cooking; 50–80 MAD per fish eater, daily, no booking.

Tue Dec 22 · casual dinner

Restaurant Zaytouna, 16 Place Chrib Atay (medina)

Mira orders the vegetarian set menu (salade marocaine or zaalouk, then tagine de légumes, about 65 MAD). Ask "bla merqa d-lham, bla hout" (no meat broth, no fish) for the tagine and any couscous, and skip the harira. Open daily 11:00–22:30; walk in.

Wed Dec 23 · lunch

Shyadma's Vegan Food, Place El Khayma / Rue Laalouj (medina)

Fully vegan, so there is no stock trap: Mira can order the tagine aux légumes, couscous aux légumes or soupe du jour, about 80 MAD. Open daily 11:00–22:30, no booking; confirm it is still trading [confirm].

Wed Dec 23 · nicer dinner

La Table by Madada, 5 Rue Youssef El Fassi (near the port)

The menu is seafood-led with no named vegetarian dish, so ask for a vegetarian plate or a seafood-free pasta when booking, and check sauces for fish or chicken stock [confirm]. Closed Tuesday, open 19:00–23:00; book (+212 524 472 106), 250–450 MAD.

Tue–Thu · breakfast

Pâtisserie Driss, 10 Rue Hajjali (Place Moulay Hassan)

Everything is vegetarian: Mira can have croissants, Moroccan pastries, msemen, orange juice and a nuss-nuss coffee. Open daily from 07:00, so it works for Thursday's mid-morning departure; no booking, about 20–50 MAD [confirm].
