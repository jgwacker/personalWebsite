Beach week for two from Phoenix: 10 plans

Sat 26 Dec 2026 to Sat 2 Jan 2027, 7 nights. Two adults, one room. All prices are cash for both. Hotel prices are Booking.com totals for the stay; flights are Google Flights round trips for two. Both checked 25 Nov 2026.

Read before booking. Rooms are going fast: 72–98% of places in each destination already show as unavailable. Booking.com totals may not include local hotel tax, which runs about 12–30% in these places (Hawaii about 19%, Cabo up to 30% with service, Turks and Caicos 12% plus 10% service), and resort fees. Food, transfer and car figures are estimates. Many luxury resorts set holiday minimum stays and charge for a New Year's Eve gala dinner.

Book through Amex Travel where you can

  • Hotels marked FHR are in Amex's Fine Hotels + Resorts program. Booked through Amex Travel with her Platinum card, each stay gets daily breakfast for two, a $100 credit, an upgrade when available, noon check-in and 4 pm check-out. That is worth roughly $600–900 over a week. Rates usually match the hotel's own flexible rate, but only show when she's logged in.
  • Several FHR hotels list extra offers (a free night, 15–25% off, a $400–750 credit). The public list doesn't show holiday blackouts, so check each one for these dates.
  • Platinum hotel credit: up to $300 off a prepaid FHR booking each half year. A booking made by 31 Dec uses this half's credit if it is unused.

How the totals are built

  • Hotel: the Booking.com total for 7 nights, two adults, one room (25 Nov). Taxes may be extra; see the note at the top.
  • Flights: Google Flights round trip for two, 26 Dec–2 Jan (25 Nov). Nonstops exist only to Mexico and Hawaii.
  • Estimates:
    • Food and drink: about $300 a day for two, $350 in Hawaii and the pricier islands. All-inclusive resorts: tips only.
    • Hawaii: a rental car, about $700 a week.
    • Elsewhere: an airport transfer.
