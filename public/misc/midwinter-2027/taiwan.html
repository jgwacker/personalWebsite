Taiwan at the Lantern Festival

The fifteenth day of the lunar year falls on Saturday February 20, 2027, the family's last night in the country. The Lunar New Year shutdown ends before you land, Taiwan is the easiest vegetarian country in Asia, and everything moves by high-speed rail. The catch is Taipei's February sky, which is grey more days than not, so the middle of the trip goes south to Tainan for sun and temples.

The photographs

In order of priority

  1. Shifen sky-lantern release at dusk, wide from the bridge or long from the edge of the square.
  2. Longshan Temple at the 06:00 opening: incense, chanting, lantern strings, no tourists.
  3. Tainan's Shennong Street and the Grand Mazu Temple at blue hour; Tainan has the densest temple district in Taiwan.
  4. Raohe night market under the Ciyou Temple gate, and Ningxia market for food-stall close-ups.
  5. Jiufen's lantern-lit stairs after 19:00 on a weekday, or Dihua Street's shophouses at dawn.

Light and weather

Taipei, mid-Feb

Sunrise about 06:28, sunset 17:47. Highs 19 C, lows 14 C, rain on 12 to 17 days, about 3 hours of sun a day.

Tainan and Kaohsiung

Sunset about ten minutes later. Highs 24 to 25 C, lows 16 C, one to three rain days, 7 to 9 hours of sun.

Eating vegetarian

About one Taiwanese in eight is vegetarian, so 素食 and 蔬食 signs are everywhere and pay-by-weight vegetarian buffets make an easy lunch. 全素 means Buddhist vegetarian, no egg, dairy, garlic or onion; 蛋奶素 means ovo-lacto.

Order. Vegetable dumplings and buns (素水餃, 素包; Din Tai Fung has them), sesame noodles without minced pork, egg crepes, soy milk with sesame flatbread and fried dough for breakfast, braised tofu and dried tofu at a 素 stall, sesame or peanut tangyuan on Lantern Festival night, douhua, taro balls in Jiufen, pineapple cake.

Traps. Pork-bone broth (大骨湯) in almost any noodle soup; minced-pork sauce (肉燥) ladled over plain vegetables in Tainan; lard in scallion pancakes and fried rice; bonito flakes on thin noodles and tofu; oyster sauce on greens; stinky tofu brine at non-vegetarian stalls; vegetable items fried in shared oil at salt-and-pepper chicken stands.

Drone and tripod

Drones over 250 g register online with the CAA on a passport, six months' validity. No flying between sunset and sunrise, 120 m ceiling, no crowds, and the Taipei basin, Tainan city and Kaohsiung sit inside controlled airspace, so sky-lantern nights and the city dawns are all off limits. Only Jiufen's coast and open countryside pencil out; leave it home. Tripods have no national rule: fine at temples off peak, useless in night markets, Elephant Mountain fills with tripods 30 minutes before sunset.

Where to stay

Bands from listings, not live quotes; pricing is the next step

Two-room suites in Taipei sit in the top tier (Mandarin Oriental, Grand Hyatt, Regent, Humble House, Kimpton) or serviced apartments near Zhongshan and Da'an, roughly US$310 to 620 a night; connecting rooms about half that. Tainan: Silks Place or Shangri-La Far Eastern suites, roughly US$220 to 370. February outside Feb 19 to 20 is low season; those two nights are a domestic travel peak.

Risks

  • Pingxi's date is unknown; the Tainan block may have to move.
  • Taipei weather: budget for four grey days and keep Tainan as the sun block.
  • Lantern-weekend HSR and hotel sell-outs around Feb 19 to 21. HSR sales open 29 days out, about Jan 22.
  • The Pingxi Line closes after storm damage; check TRA a day or two before.
  • Only an early Friday departure buys an eighth night, and that means leaving school early.
