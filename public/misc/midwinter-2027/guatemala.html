Antigua and Lake Atitlán

Antigua at six in the morning with Agua volcano behind the arch, Fuego glowing at night from a rooftop, Chichicastenango's Thursday market on the church steps, weavers' cooperatives on the lake: all of it is dry, sunny and photogenic in February. The problem is the single road between Antigua and the lake, an honest three hours by shuttle, which breaks the family's two-hour rule in both directions unless it is split at Tecpán with lunch and the Iximché ruins in between.

Verdict. Marginal on the drive rule, no nonstop in February, a Level 3 advisory. It passes only if two three-hour car days with a lunch stop are acceptable. Otherwise the one-base alternative is Antigua alone, which is a smaller trip.

The photographs

In order of priority

  1. Chichicastenango on Thursday Feb 18: incense and flower sellers on the Santo Tomás steps at eight. No photos inside the church; ask before portraits.
  2. Antigua at blue hour: Calle del Arco with Agua behind it at six, Fuego's plume from Cerro de la Cruz once the tourist police are on duty.
  3. Santiago Atitlán and San Juan La Laguna: backstrap weaving, natural-dye cooperatives, women's traje, Maximón's shrine.
  4. Sololá's Friday market.
  5. Lake dawn: mist and three volcanoes from a Santa Cruz or Santa Catarina dock, lanchas loading at six.

Light and weather

Antigua

Sunrise about 06:25, sunset 18:05. Highs 26 C, lows 13 C, dawn feels like 10. Chichicastenango and Sololá sit near 2,000 m and are cold at eight.

Haze

Agricultural burning builds from February; volcano views are best at first light.

Eating vegetarian

Easy in Antigua, where several restaurants make plant-based pepián; harder in the villages and market comedores, where 'sin carne' often means no beef and chicken still appears.

Order. Frijoles volteados after asking about lard, plátanos fritos, huevos rancheros, dobladas and tostadas with cheese or guacamole, rellenitos de plátano, chiles rellenos de verduras, atol de elote. Antigua: Samsara, La Bruja, 27 Adentro, El Comalote.

Traps. Pepián and jocón (chicken broth), kak'ik (turkey), chuchitos, paches and tamales (lard in the masa, meat inside), caldos and sopa de tortilla, rice cooked in consomé, refried beans in manteca.

Drone and tripod

Email the DGAC form before arrival; the approved form also clears customs. 400 ft, line of sight, no night flight, insurance, and no flying over gatherings of a hundred or more without a special permit, so no market and no procession. It earns its weight only for lake-shore dawns. Tripods: no published restrictions anywhere on the route; cobblestones are the practical limit.

Where to stay

Bands from listings, not live quotes; pricing is the next step

Antigua: Casa Santo Domingo suites about US$400 to 520 with breakfast and a rooftop volcano view; El Convento and Casa Bella suites US$200 to 300. Most Antigua suites are one big room, so ask for two rooms or connecting rooms. Lake: Hotel Atitlán in Panajachel has a true two-bedroom family suite at about US$200 to 300 next to the Chichicastenango shuttle; Casa Palopó suites from about US$400; Laguna Lodge in Santa Cruz is boat-access with a vegetarian kitchen, US$140 to 260.

Risks

  • Even the split version is about three hours of car on two days.
  • A repeat of the August 2026 Fuego activity would close Acatenango and could ash out Antigua.
  • No nonstop; a missed Houston connection costs a day.
  • US State Department Level 3 since March 2026; the Level 4 zones are off this route. Practical risks are pickpockets in markets, Cerro de la Cruz outside police hours, and afternoon lanchas, one of which capsized in January 2026.
  • Haze softening the volcano views.
