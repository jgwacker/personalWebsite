Hokkaido in Winter

United starts a nonstop from San Francisco to Sapporo in December 2026, flying Fridays, which lands the family in Hokkaido on Saturday morning with eight nights to spend. The photographs on offer exist nowhere else: red-crowned cranes dancing in the steam of the Setsuri River at minus fifteen, whooper swans in hot-spring mist on a frozen lake, drift ice and sea eagles from an icebreaker's deck. Every headline festival ends the week before you arrive, so this is a wildlife trip with Sapporo and Tokyo as bookends.

Verdict. The highest photographic ceiling of the six and the hardest trip: three internal legs, dawns at minus fifteen to minus twenty, a crane village with no suites, and the hardest country here for a vegetarian. Worth it if the cranes are the point.

The photographs

In order of priority

  1. Cranes in the Setsuri River steam from Otowa Bridge: arrive 05:30, civil dawn 05:50, sunrise 06:18, tripod to tripod.
  2. Dancing pairs and flight at the Tsurui-Ito or Tsurumidai feedings.
  3. Whooper swans in hot-spring steam at Sunayu with lake ice behind them.
  4. Drift ice and Steller's sea eagles from the Aurora's deck off Abashiri.
  5. Otaru canal's gas lamps at blue hour, snow on the warehouses.

Sapporo, Feb 17

Sunrise 06:30, sunset 17:07. Highs -1 to 0 C, lows -8 C, snow about one day in three, metre-deep snowpack.

Eating vegetarian

Hokkaido is harder than Tokyo. Bonito dashi is in miso soup, soba and udon broth, tamagoyaki, inari rice, tempura dipping sauce, okonomiyaki batter and most vegetable soups; soup curry and ramen are pork or chicken bone.

Order. Zaru soba only where the shop confirms konbu-only tsuyu; vegetable tempura with salt instead of sauce; yudofu; onigiri with umeboshi or konbu (not okaka or tuna mayo); kappa and natto maki; jaga-butter, grilled corn, Hokkaido cheese and soft-serve. Indian and Nepali curry houses are the reliable dinner everywhere. Sapporo: Veggy Way miso ramen, Lohas, Xi Fang. Tokyo Station: T's Tantan.

Traps. Anything with broth unless asked; 'vegetable' soups; tempura sauce; inari; the Tsurui inns' set dinners, which need to be arranged in advance.

Drone and tripod

Anything over 100 g must be registered on DIPS 2.0 two weeks ahead, and central Tokyo, Sapporo, Kanazawa and Kushiro are densely-inhabited no-fly districts without a separate permit. Assume no flying near cranes, swans or in the national parks. Leave it home. Tripods are fine at Otowa Bridge, Tsurui-Ito, Sunayu, the Otaru canal and Abashiri; banned at the Shirakawa-go viewpoint, Tokyo Tower and Mori Tower; Skytree allows them.

Where to stay

Bands from listings, not live quotes; pricing is the next step

Sapporo: Holiday Inn & Suites Odori Park (opened 2025, fourteen maisonette suites with a separate bedroom) or JR Tower Hotel Nikko suites, roughly US$200 to 400 a night for three. Tsurui has no suites: Hotel Taito and Hickory Wind are photographers' inns with tatami family rooms and meals, roughly US$270 to 400; the alternative is an ANA Crowne Plaza Kushiro suite and 45-minute pre-dawn rides. Abashiri: Lake Abashiri Tsuruga Resort, half board. Tokyo: Mimaru Suites two-bedroom apartments.

Risks

  • Weather cancels the icebreaker or the Senmo line trains; cranes may not dance on a given morning.
  • Three internal legs and an open-jaw ticket; United's Sapporo route is in its first season.
  • Cold kills camera batteries and a middle-schooler's morale; the dawns are the whole point and they are brutal.
  • The Honshu alternative (Tokyo, snow monkeys, Takayama, Kanazawa, all by rail) is bombproof but has nothing unique in this window.
