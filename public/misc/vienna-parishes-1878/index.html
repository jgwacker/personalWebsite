In 1878 the city of Vienna was only districts 1 to 10; Favoriten had become the 10th district in 1874. The rest were separate villages and market towns in Lower Austria, absorbed in 1892 (districts 11–19), 1900 (Brigittenau), 1904 (Floridsdorf and the left bank) and 1938 (Liesing). All of them belonged to the Archdiocese of Vienna, so their registers sit together on Matricula, and a Vienna-born child of 1878 could have been baptised in any of them. Hospitals and foundling homes with their own registers, above all the General Hospital in the Alsergrund, baptised a large share of the city's illegitimate children.

Maria Stich, 1878. Rennweg Maria Geburt, Taufbuch 01-16, p. 245, no. 377: Hermine Maria Stich, born 24 July 1878 at Schützengasse 10, baptised 31 July 1878, legitimate. Father Johann Georg Stich, Lutheran, innkeeper, born at Eschenbach in Bavaria, son of Konrad Stich, cottager, and Magdalena née Härlein. Mother Josefa née Andre, Catholic, born in Vienna, daughter of Franz Andre, master watch-case maker, and Anna née Hager. Godmother Maria Stich, innkeeper, Erdbergerstrasse 50. The parents had married in Vienna's Lutheran church on 18 June 1878. The same family had a son Leopold baptised here in 1877 and a son Josef in 1879. Register page on Matricula.

Maria Stich, 1879. Mariahilf, Taufbuch 01-19, fol. 285, no. 97: Maria Magdalena, born 29 April 1879 at Mariahilferstrasse 46, baptised 1 May 1879, illegitimate, mother Maria Anna Stich (daughter of Ferdinand Stich, cottager, and Evangelika(?) née Bernhart), a seamstress. Legitimated on 24 August 1888 by Sebastian Graser, servant at the Unionbank, son of Maternus(?) Graser, farmer, and Rosina née Lenk; the parents had married on 22 July 1888 in the Rossau parish. A later note records her death on 27 October 1955 at Versorgungsheimplatz 1, Vienna 13 (the Lainz home). Godmother Magdalena Pich(?); midwife Anna Dirl, Lerchenfelderstrasse 126. Register page on Matricula.

Result of the full sweep. All 79 parish indexes were read for 1877–1879. Exactly one girl surnamed Stich with Maria in her name was baptised in Vienna in 1878: Hermine Maria at Rennweg. The two other 1878 “Maria Stich” entries in MyHeritage's index do not match any parish register; the likeliest source is the 1879 Mariahilf baptism, or a register Matricula does not hold, such as the foundling home's or a Protestant parish's.
