Ski week from Phoenix: 10 plans

Sat 26 Dec 2026 to Sat 2 Jan 2027 (7 nights) unless marked. Two adults and a 15-year-old, in two rooms or a suite. All prices are cash for all three people. Hotel prices are Booking.com totals for the stay; flights are Google Flights round trips for three. Both checked 25 Nov 2026.

Draft. These are live prices as of 25 Nov, but rooms are going fast: 81–99% of places in each resort already show as unavailable. Booking.com totals may not include every local tax, so check them on the property page. Lift, rental, transfer and food figures are estimates from published 2026/27 rates.

Act this week

  • Lift tickets:
    • Epic passes stop selling after Sun 6 Dec. A 5-day Epic Day Pass with holiday access is about $700 each. It covers Whistler, Vail, Beaver Creek and Park City, against $325–392 a day at the window.
    • Vail Resorts' 4-weeks-ahead ticket discount ends about 28 Nov.
    • Aspen's 30-day advance price ends about 26 Nov.
  • Book FHR hotels through Amex Travel with her Platinum card (Fine Hotels + Resorts, marked FHR below). Each room gets daily breakfast for two, a $100 credit, an upgrade when available and 4 pm check-out, for up to three rooms on one booking. Two rooms means breakfast for all three, worth roughly $800–1,000 over the week. Amex prices only show when she's logged in, so they are not shown here; they usually match the hotel's own flexible rate.
  • Platinum hotel credit: up to $300 off a prepaid FHR booking each half year. If this half's credit is unused, a booking made by 31 Dec uses it.

Each location shows the hotel I would pick, with the alternatives indented below it. Every hotel line has its own all-in total for three: hotel, flights, airport transfer, 5-day lift tickets, ski rental and food. Locations are ranked by value for a high-end week, not by price alone.

How the totals are built

  • Hotel: the Booking.com total for 7 nights, three adults, two rooms or one suite (25 Nov). Grand Hyatt Deer Valley uses Hyatt's own rate.
  • Lifts: 5 days for three. Epic resorts: Epic Day Pass with holiday access, ~$700 each. Ikon resorts: online advance prices. Europe: 6-day passes with teen rates.
  • Estimates:
    • Ski rental: about $1,200 for three in North America, $600–700 in Europe.
    • Food: about $300 a day for three in North America; less in Europe or where meals are included.
    • Transfers: private car or shuttle, from published ranges.
